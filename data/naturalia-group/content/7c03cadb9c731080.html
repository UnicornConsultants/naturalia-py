Better improvement. Better future.


Established in 2009, Lumiva International Beauty Academy is dedicated to provide excellent professional beauty, nutritional and aesthetic training courses to all sectors of society, so as to promote and enhance their awareness of health and nutrition. All members are eligible to gain a Diploma from British Association of Beauty Therapy and Cosmetology (CIBTAC) after finishing the whole course. Apart from beauty specialists or people in health-related industry who are required to study nutrition courses, courses with different durations and levels are also available for those who are interested in gaining nutrition knowledge to join.

Lumiva International Beauty Academy has enrolled diploma courses with international recognition, including Diploma in Diet and Nutrition recognized by CIBTAC, Bridging course for Diploma in Nutrition and Nutrition Certificate Programmes. All enrolled courses are examined and designed by registered nutritionists who have received professional trainings. The duration of a course is approximately 50 hours (42-hour theory and 8-hour practice). Diploma in Nutrition from British Association of Beauty Therapy and Cosmetology (CIBTAC) and Lumiva International Beauty Academy will be issued for students who have attended at least 80% of the formal curriculum and passed the examination and class practices.

About CIBTAC

CIBTAC is one of the longest established awarding bodies within the health and beauty sector. Working with our sister company British Association of Beauty Therapy and Cosmetology (BABTAC), CIBTAC is also a representative of CIDESCO in the UK. CIBTAC Certificates are well-recognized in the globe. Graduates are well-trained, have the knowledge to safely carry out treatments independently and have the understanding and expertise to start adding their own flair to treatments as soon as they start working.They are the precious resources of corporates.
