Corporate Social Responsibility


Yuntao Group's Corporate Social Responsibility

Yuntao believes that Corporate Social Responsibility (CSR) is not merely about participating in charitable initiatives, but rather stems from a sincere commitment to contributing selflessly to society and the environment. Built on a foundation of over twenty years, we focus our efforts on four core areas to make multifaceted contributions to our employees, the community, the business sector, and the ecological environment.

Employees
Providing a safe and comfortable working environment is an expression of our responsibility towards our employees and their families. Yuntao is dedicated to ensuring a secure and pleasant workplace, protecting employees from discrimination, and providing ample support. For a decade, the Group has upheld the philosophy of "People First, Rooted in Professionalism," offering employees comprehensive training and career development opportunities to help them fully realize their potential. Furthermore, Yuntao regards employees as family; our relationship transcends that of employer and employee, evolving into one of kinship and friendship. We grow together, support one another, and achieve our shared aspirations.

Community Care
Yuntao has consistently and quietly dedicated itself to the community, fostering a harmonious and compassionate business environment and giving back to society. The Group encourages its departments to collaborate with local organizations to plan and execute activities tailored to the needs of various districts. We also support our employees in actively participating in volunteer work to show care for society. As the Group continues to grow, we are committed to making even greater contributions to the community.

Ecological Environment
Yuntao implements various environmental protection measures and promotes environmental awareness in multiple ways to support sustainable development. We maintain air conditioning at 25°C to conserve energy and reduce greenhouse gas emissions. Simultaneously, we insist on double-sided printing and paper recycling whenever possible to minimize paper waste, protect trees, and lessen our impact on the natural environment. We actively support environmental initiatives and continuously advocate for eco-consciousness among our employees and clients. Since the global "Earth Hour" campaign began in 2008, Yuntao has participated annually, doing its part for environmental protection.

Market Relations
Yuntao strives to propel market development and lead the industry forward. Recognizing that market demands and customer preferences are constantly evolving, the Group established the Yuntao Healthy Skin Management Association in 2014. This association regularly organizes voluntary educational sessions and provides professional training for industry practitioners to support the sector. Guided by a customer-first ethos, the Group continuously introduces high-quality and safe products and services. Our medical aesthetic products and equipment undergo clinical verification and fully comply with international standards, meeting the certifications of the U.S. Food and Drug Administration (FDA) and the European Union Medical Device Safety Certification (CE0476). We also actively gather customer feedback through various channels to ensure even greater satisfaction.
