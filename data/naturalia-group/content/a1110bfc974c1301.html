Leader's Message
      

Mr. Chan Kin Wah 
Chairman of Naturalia Group Holdings Limited  
Founding President of the Global Healthy Skin Management Association

With over 30 years of entrepreneurship, Mr. Chan Kin Wah, Chairman of Naturalia Group Holdings Limited, has dedicated his career to the health and beauty industry. As the first to introduce medical aesthetics to Mainland China more than a decade ago, his pioneering spirit has established one legendary milestone after another in the business world. As the founder of Naturalia Healthy Skin Management Center and the initiator of the Global Healthy Skin Management Association, Mr. Chan has solidified his position as a benchmark in the industry.

His forward-thinking vision, acute crisis awareness, and exceptional counterintuitive thinking serve as powerful tools that keep him ahead of the industry and resilient amid economic fluctuations. By proposing an "Entity Finance" operational strategy, he distinguished the Group's business from the market, instantly elevating the brand's value and status, and carving out a unique path in the beauty industry.

In corporate management, Mr. Chan promotes a corporate culture centered on "Activating Positive Thinking, Transforming into a Growth Mindset, and Creating Lifelong Wealth."He adheres to three core principles: Wisdom, Freedom, and the Path of Truth, fully empowering employees to exercise independent thinking and autonomy. He encourages a work style where staff dare to speak up, take action, and shoulder responsibilities.

Staying at the forefront of the era and mastering the pulse of development, Mr. Chan led Naturalia Group in pioneering the transition to technology-driven beauty—another innovative move in building a beauty empire. Core technological value, combined with a self-developed management system, forms the solid foundation that keeps Naturalia Group unshakable in the market, enabling it to build the industry's most robust platform and a unique human brand.
