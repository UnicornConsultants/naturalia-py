Efficacy: Anti-pigmentation treatment, whitening and brightening, anti-aging treatment, improves dull and sallow skin tone, moisturizes, and removes blemishes


Usage Instructions: (Salon / Professional Treatment)

Cleansing + Toning: Cleanse with an ultra-gentle cleansing cream and rinse thoroughly.

Exfoliation Phase (Removal of Dead Cells): For sensitive skin, the therapist may skip exfoliation or shorten the application time as appropriate. Gently pat a few drops of Skin Refining Serum.

Apply a thin layer (3-5ml) of Delight 3PRO Step 1 Exfoliating Product using a fan brush. Leave on for 5 minutes. If necessary, gently massage targeted areas with fingertips before rinsing.

Cleanse / Rinse: Rinse thoroughly.

Balancing Parameters (pH + Natural Moisturizing Factors):
Apply a thin layer of Special Vanishing Cream to restore pH levels using a gentle patting motion. Then, use gauze to gently pat on the Special Moisturizing Complex to restore Natural Moisturizing Factors (NMF)

Note: This step must be performed for the first 4 treatments. Thereafter, it may be performed at the therapist's discretion. Apply 0.5ml of Delight Dark Spot Corrector only on pigmented areas until absorbed.

Melanin Control Phase: Apply 5ml of Delight 3PRO Step 2 Gel Activator over the entire face using light brushing motions. Leave on for 5 minutes.

Boosting Phase: Apply 10ml of Delight 3PRO Step 3 Treatment Mask using a brush. Leave on for 7–10 minutes, during which finger pressure massage may be performed. After the time has elapsed, allow the remaining mask to be absorbed through anti-aging massage. Finally, gently pat off any residue with a slightly damp cotton pad.

Final Step: Apply a layer of Multi-Protection Cream to defend against environmental aggressors.

Treatment Cycle:

  • It is recommended to first undergo at least 4–5 treatments (as one course), once per week.

  • Thereafter, have 1 treatment every 15 days (4 treatments per course).

  • Finally, have 1 maintenance treatment per month.

Note: For pigmentation issues, the complete professional treatment protocol must be followed.
