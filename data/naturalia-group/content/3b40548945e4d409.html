III. Shipping & Delivery Information

Processing Time:
After you place an order (excluding the order date itself), products typically take approximately **7 business days** to ship out. This does not include public holidays and weekends.

Delivery Hours:
Our delivery hours are from Monday to Friday, 10:00 AM - 6:00 PM. No deliveries are scheduled on weekends or public holidays.

Potential Delays:
Please note that processing times may be extended during holidays, periods of high order volume, or due to force majeure events such as natural disasters. We appreciate your understanding in these circumstances.
