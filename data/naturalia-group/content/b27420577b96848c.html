Shirley Price Aromatherapy (UK)

With a history of over 45 years

Founded in the UK, Shirley Price Aromatherapy has established itself as a high-quality, professional essential oil brand. The founder, Shirley Price, is a respected author of multiple books on aromatherapy.

The Shirley Price story began in 1974 in the UK. In 1978, she founded the Shirley Price International College of Aromatherapy, solidifying the brand's reputation as a trusted name in aromatherapy education and a supplier of essential oils and aromatherapy products.

Recognized by the IFPA and used by colleges.

The Core Philosophy
While aromatherapy is not a medicine, its goal within a therapeutic context is prevention—it works by stimulating the circulatory and immune systems. The plant-derived essential oils you find are natural and typically free from harmful side effects. Most importantly, incorporating these oils into our daily lives can help us live happier and more fulfilling lives.
