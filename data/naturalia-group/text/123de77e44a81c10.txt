Naturalia Sintesi
Founded in 1976, Naturalia Sintesi originated from the Italian pharmaceutical company Bergamon SPA and is a pioneer in bio-scientific skincare products, utilizing biotechnology for dermatological research and development.
Safety, Efficacy, and European Standards
Naturalia Sintesi prioritizes both safety and effectiveness by minimizing the use of preservatives and fragrances in its formulations. Throughout the production and testing phases, the performance and skin tolerance of its products for all skin types adhere to stringent European standards.
A Commitment to Aesthetics and Education
Beyond product development, Naturalia Sintesi places a strong emphasis on aesthetics. It established one of the first schools of inspirational aesthetics in Rome, imparting professional knowledge and business operational skills based on the teaching philosophy of 'dell'Ortodermia'.
Personalized Support and Global Reach
Furthermore, Naturalia Sintesi provides one-on-one technical support, emphasizing versatility and flexibility, and assists operators in understanding the aesthetics and economic management of their institutes.
Today, the Naturalia Sintesi brand, representing the quality of "Made in Italy," has expanded its presence to China, Malaysia, Spain, Canada, and Taiwan.
