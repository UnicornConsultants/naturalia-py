Membership and Shopping Credit Issuance
Upon successful purchase and completion of payment, the system will automatically send the shopping credit corresponding to the membership fee to the account and manually upgrade to the corresponding wholesale membership level. Customers can check their current membership level information in the member center.
Shopping Credit Validity Period
Shopping credit is valid for 180 days from the date of issuance. Any unused shopping credit will automatically expire after the validity period and will not be reissued or extended.
Membership Renewal Method
If the shopping credit is used up, a top-up must be completed within 7 days after the reminder notification to maintain the wholesale membership qualification. If no top-up is made within the time limit, the system will automatically downgrade the account to a regular member.

For any refunds issued, the customer is responsible for bearing the handling fee costs incurred by the respective payment method.
