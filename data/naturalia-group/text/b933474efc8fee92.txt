Corporate Profile
Promoting professional, scientific, and rigorous correct skincare concepts, and advocating a lifestyle of healthy beauty
Naturalia Group Holdings Limited (NATURALIA GROUP) is a reputable and well-established health management group renowned in the Asia-Pacific region. It leads the industry with its high-quality products, services, and team management, setting the highest standards for the sector. Currently, the Group has established branches and subsidiaries in Hong Kong, Macau, Greater China, and Southeast Asia.
Driven by its mission to spread health and beauty, Naturalia Group not only provides safe and effective products to the market but also offers partners the most efficient, safest, and most reliable one-stop professional services. These include rigorous professional management training, superior product training, highly effective marketing and sales techniques, and thoughtful customer service skills. As of June 2015, Naturalia has assisted over 3,800 beauty enterprises in achieving rapid development and breakthroughs.
As a responsible member of society, Naturalia Group is committed to fulfilling its corporate social responsibilities and giving back to the community. Since late 2014, adhering to the philosophy of "**People-Oriented, Professionally-Rooted**," Naturalia Group has collaborated with authoritative medical professors from the European Monaco Monte Carlo Medical Association to promote the establishment of a professional non-profit organization—the Global Healthy Skin Management Association. Through professional exchanges and learning, the association aims to bring the world's most advanced medical beauty technology and information to every member, with the primary goal of advancing the development of the health and medical beauty industry in the Asia-Pacific region, disseminating professional, scientific, and rigorous correct skincare concepts, and promoting a lifestyle of healthy beauty.
