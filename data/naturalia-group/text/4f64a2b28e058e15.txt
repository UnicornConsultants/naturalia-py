Efficacy: This product is specifically formulated for treating facial acne, excess toxins, enlarged pores, and blackheads
Usage instructions:
Salon / Professional use: It is recommended to first use Carotene to remove dead skin, then begin the treatment kit.
No. 1 Balancing Cream – Apply the entire amount to the face using a brush. Leave on for five minutes, then gently pat the remaining amount into the skin until fully absorbed.
Comedone extraction (needle cleaning) for acne and blackheads – to be performed at the therapist's discretion.
Apply a few drops of TOP LINE onto acne and comedone areas. Wait three minutes.
No. 2 Treatment Serum – Apply to the entire face and neck. Leave on for five minutes without wiping off, allowing it to absorb. For better results, use in combination with YOUNG AGAIN blue light therapy.
No. 3 Repairing Mask – Apply the entire amount evenly over the entire treated area. Leave on for 15–20 minutes, then wipe off.
Finally, apply a thin layer of DNA Balancing Sensitive Cream.
Note: A minimum of 8 treatments is recommended, once every 5 days. For every 4 PUFAC treatments, incorporate one additional hydrating repair / skin regeneration treatment. In the morning, use DNA Balancing Sensitive Cream or sunscreen (avoid unprotected exposure to UV rays).
