How We Use "Cookies" and Other Similar Tracking Technologies
(1) What are "Cookies"
Cookies are small files stored on your computer, mobile phone, or any other smart terminal device by the Store's server when you log in to the Store or browse the Store's content. They usually contain an identifier, the Store's name, and some numbers and characters. When you visit the Store again, the Store can recognize your browser through the Cookie. Cookies may store user preferences and other information.
(2) How We Use "Cookies"
When you use our Store, we may collect information such as the device model, operating system, device identifier, and login IP address of the data subject through Cookies or similar technologies, and cache the data subject's browsing information and click information to understand the data subject's network environment. Cookies allow us to recognize your identity when visiting the Store, continuously optimize the user-friendliness of the Store, and adjust the Store according to your needs. You can also change your browser settings so that the browser does not accept Cookies from our Store, but this may affect your use of certain functions of the Store.
On the Store we have established via SHOPLINE, with the help of Cookies and other similar technologies, we can identify whether you are an existing user or member without requiring re-login and authentication on each page.
(3) How to Manage "Cookies"
You can manage or delete certain categories of tracking technologies based on your preferences. Many browsers have a "Do Not Track" feature that can send a "Do Not Track" request to the Store.
In addition to the controls we provide, you can also choose to enable or disable cookies in your internet browser. Most internet browsers also allow you to choose whether to disable all cookies or only third-party cookies. By default, most internet browsers accept cookies, but this setting can be changed. For more details, please see the "Help" menu in your internet browser or your device's documentation.
The following links provide instructions on how to control cookies in all major browsers:
* Google Chrome: https://support.google.com/chrome/answer/95647?hl=en
* Internet Explorer: https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/help/260971/description-of-cookies
* Safari (Desktop): https://support.apple.com/kb/PH5042?locale=en_US
* Mozilla Firefox: https://support.mozilla.org/en-US/kb/cookies-information-websites-store-on-your-computer?redirectlocale=en-US&redirectslug=Cookies
* Opera: https://www.opera.com/help
If you use any other browser, please refer to the documentation provided by the browser.
On the Store, you can delete existing tracking technologies by clearing the cache.
When you browse the web without logging in, we collect the Cookies necessary to enable browsing functions to provide you with relevant services.
Please note that if you refuse to use or delete existing tracking technologies, you will need to manually change user settings each time you visit, we may not be able to provide you with a quality user experience, and some functions may not work properly.
